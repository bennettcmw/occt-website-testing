OCC Transport’s Training Program

Description

Our training program is a six-week intensive program designed to develop qualified bus drivers. Participants earn a New York State Class B Commercial Driver’s License and receive a completion stipend after successfully completing the training program.

Program Overview

Our program curriculum features 3–4 three-hour driving sessions plus one classroom hour per week. Flexible scheduling is available. The NYS CDL Class B Road Test takes place in week four of training. All training sessions are mandatory.

Hiring Policy

OCC Transport is an Equal Opportunity Employer. Hiring decisions are based on demonstrated driving competency, rule compliance, and passage of the road test.

Applicant Requirements

  • Minimum one year of active, licensed U.S. driving experience
  • Clean driving record — zero active points or violations
  • DOT medical and physical clearance
  • Enrolled at Binghamton University for a minimum of 6–7 remaining semesters
