CS

Campus Shuttle

Shuttle service serving all residential communities, parking lots, and key academic buildings — two loops operating around campus

Route Stops & Schedule

The Outer Loop runs as a continuous loop, serving all residential communities as well as the Engineering Building, Decker Health Services, the East and West Gyms, the Events Center, Clearview, and Academic A. On weekday evenings and weekends, service runs on a fixed schedule.
Important: Buses departing from the Engineering Building do not serve Mohawk, and buses departing from Mohawk do not serve the Engineering Building. These are separate loops — board at the correct stop for your destination.

Schedule
Daytime: The Outer Loop runs as a continuous loop from 7:00 AM to 9:40 PM with no fixed timetable. Check ETA SPOT for real-time arrival estimates.
After 9:40 PM: The continuous loop ends. All campus shuttle service operates as blue buses departing on scheduled, non-contiguous loops from Mohawk only. See the evening schedule below.

Passenger Notices

Engineering Bldg ↔ Mohawk (Outer Loop): These are separate loops. Buses from Engineering Building do not stop at Mohawk, and buses from Mohawk do not stop at Engineering Building. Board at the correct stop.
Inner Loop — Weekdays Only: The Inner Loop runs Mon–Fri during the day only. No Inner Loop service on evenings, weekends, or holidays.
After 9:40 PM (weekdays): The Outer Loop continuous service ends. All service switches to blue buses on scheduled, non-contiguous loops departing from Mohawk only.
Weekends: All campus shuttles run as blue buses on scheduled, non-contiguous loops departing from Mohawk only. No Engineering Building departures and no Inner Loop service.
