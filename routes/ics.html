ITC-Campus Shuttle

Seasonal shuttle connecting the Innovative Technologies Complex to residential communities — intersessions & breaks only

Route Stops & Schedule

The ITC-Campus Shuttle is a seasonal Green Bus service running a continuous loop between Mohawk, the Innovative Technologies Complex, Hillside, and all stops in between. It operates weekdays only, 8:00 AM–6:00 PM, and only when campus is in a Summer Intersession, Winter Intersession, or academic break (Spring Break, Fall Break). It does not run during the regular semester.
Not sure if ICS is running today? Check News & Info or confirm on the Routes & Schedules page. During the regular semester this route is suspended and normal campus shuttle service applies.

Passenger Notices

Seasonal service only. ICS does not operate during the regular academic semester. Service runs during Summer & Winter Intersessions and on a reduced schedule for Spring & Fall Breaks.
Weekdays only. No ICS service on Saturdays, Sundays, or university holidays.
Green Buses only. This route is served exclusively by Green Shuttle buses, not Blue Buses.
